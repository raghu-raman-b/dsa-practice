Opening move — the rehearsal

read statement → find the giveaway → read constraints → fix the complexity budget → then choose the pattern. Most people choose the pattern first and back-fill the budget, which is how they write O(n²) for n ≤ 10⁵.

Escalation ladder

(a) leading question → (b) nudge at the invariant → (c) the idea in plain words → (d) code, only on explicit surrender. On timeout with no hint used: editorial idea in three lines, log trigger→pattern, move on. Grinding is the wrong muscle.

Graded triage first, code second

Ordering · over-investment (>35% on one problem) · left on the table · reading cost. A fully solved paper with bad allocation still grades poorly — the allocation is what generalises. Then recognition, then a brief code review, then one prescription.

§3

Standing rules

These don't bend for a good session or a bad one.

  1. Every problem timed. Exceeded the box ⇒ auto-burn, regardless of verdict.
  2. Timer expires ⇒ read the editorial, log trigger→pattern, move on. Do not grind.
  3. Mistake log entry for every failure: trigger → pattern, one line.
  4. progress.md updated by hand from the close block at the end of every session. A stale file is worse than none — it looks current.
  5. Anki reviewed daily. Hard cap ~250 cards across both decks.
  6. Weekly rated contest — better pressure simulation, and the rating is the one verifiable number for the CV.
  7. Optimal not visible in ~5 minutes ⇒ write and submit the brute force first. Partial credit is real credit.
  8. Constraints → complexity budget, read before writing anything.
  9. Never trust a recalled question list — yours or a model's. Verify numbers against titles; verify company sets against LeetCode Discuss.

bare.h — after the heap build (~25 Aug), one no-STL header: MyStack, MyQueue, MyHeap, MyHashMap, plus BIT and segment tree. Own names, never shadowing STL identifiers. Night-window tactics. Bright light 18:00–20:00 holds the delayed phase. One caffeine dose at 17:30–18:00, nothing after 20:00 — the half-life is ~5–6h and it degrades sleep depth even when you fall asleep fine. Same wake time every day including off days; a three-hour weekend swing re-breaks the adaptation every Monday. One full off day per week — not a light day. Eat with people at least once a day; a night-shifted schedule isolates you fast.
